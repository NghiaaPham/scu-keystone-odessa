Our approach

  • Primary sources first. County plans, GIS layers, traffic data, studies, staff reports and Board minutes — cited by document, date and page.
  • Sources beside the fact. Each figure on the site carries a short source line, with the full method here.
  • Dated, not live. Snapshots are tied to the date the data was taken. A dated count can change when the County updates its records.
  • Illustrative content is labeled. Sample figures, library records and testimonials used for layout are marked “Illustrative — pending verification” until verified.
  • Corrections are public. Changes to published facts are recorded in the Edits log.
02

Key figures and how they were produced

34.3%8,025 of 23,387 acres

Conservation land and water in the Community Plan area

Share of the adopted Keystone–Odessa Community Plan area that is mapped conservation land, lakes, reservoirs or ponds.

  1. Start from the official, adopted Community Plan boundary (County GIS).
  2. Combine conservation land and mapped lakes, reservoirs and ponds from Plan Hillsborough, FNAI (September 2026), acquired ELAPP lands and SWFWMD (2023).
  3. Clip each layer to the Plan boundary and merge overlaps so every acre is counted once.
  4. Divide the merged area (8,025 acres) by the boundary area (23,387 acres).

GIS measurement, October 16, 2026. Conservation and water layers describe different protections and ownership; they are not all government-owned land.

4,542improved single-family parcels · 49.5% under one acre

Residential parcels in the Plan area

Analysis of the October 2, 2026 County GIS parcel snapshot. 2,249 of the 4,542 selected parcels report less than one acre.

  1. Count unique tax folios, so each parcel appears once.
  2. Assign a parcel to the official boundary when its interior point falls inside it.
  3. Keep parcels with a single-family classification, a positive building value and at least one recorded building.
  4. Use the full reported parcel acreage for the size comparison.

The October 2 snapshot is dated, not a live count. Parcels are not a count of occupied homes.

19,912vehicle movements a day, 2024 AADT

Traffic on Gunn Highway

2024 annual-average daily traffic on Gunn Highway from the Pasco County line to Van Dyke Road (County traffic layer, OBJECTID 582). Two lanes; daily and peak-direction level of service E against a standard of D.

For comparison, Van Dyke Road from Gunn Highway to the Suncoast north ramp carried 17,500 (OBJECTID 136), also two lanes and LOS E against D.

County 2024 level-of-service traffic sections. AADT measures vehicle movements, not unique people or injuries.

04

What these figures do not show

  • Traffic counts measure vehicle movements, not people, crashes or injuries.
  • Parcel counts are not a count of occupied homes or residents.
  • Mapped conservation and water layers come from different agencies and describe different kinds of protection.
  • Engineering guidance from federal agencies is general; it is not a finding about a specific Keystone–Odessa road.
  • No figure on this site predicts the outcome of retiring the Community Plan. Outcomes depend on future County decisions made in public.

Spotted an error or a better source? Tell us — confirmed corrections are listed in the Edits log.
