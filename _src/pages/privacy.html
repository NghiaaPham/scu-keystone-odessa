03

Separate choices, separate consents

  • Joining the supporter network, signing the petition and subscribing to email updates are three separate choices. One never signs you up for another.
  • Email updates are optional and start unchecked. Leaving the box unchecked never prevents you from signing the petition.
  • We do not import consent from one form into another.
  • Please do not send us other people's personal information without their permission.
04

The petition and public records

By signing, I support the request above. I understand that this petition may be submitted to public agencies and that my printed name, community or ZIP code, signature, and date may become public records.

Because petition details may become public records, we cannot promise confidentiality or anonymity for them. We will not publish signers' contact details, and signing does not give permission to display your name on this website.
