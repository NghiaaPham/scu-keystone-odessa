About Safer Communities United

Neighbors asking for a plan that puts people first.

Safer Communities United is a Florida nonprofit civic organization working to protect the health, safety, and welfare of the people of Keystone-Odessa.

Keystone–Odessa, northwest Hillsborough County, Florida

Our mission

Putting the health, safety, and welfare of people first.

We educate the public and advocate for a lawful County process to retire the Keystone-Odessa Community Plan and apply the otherwise applicable policies of the Hillsborough County Comprehensive Plan.

Existing homes, divided lots and major roads are part of Keystone–Odessa today. The decisions that shape it — roads, water and septic, housing, access to everyday services — should start from the safety and well-being of the people who live here and travel through.

We are residents, property owners, workers and community supporters. We value the landscape as much as anyone. We also believe a planning document adopted in 2001 should be judged by what it does today, using current evidence.

What we ask

Retire the separate local plan. Keep the protections.

Our petition to the Hillsborough County Board of County Commissioners asks the County to begin a Comprehensive Plan amendment, with all legally required analysis, notice and public hearings.

  1. Remove the Community Plan and its associated policies

    Including the implementing Land Development Code provisions, Part 3.08.00 (Keystone-Odessa Rural Development Standards), through the required public processes — while retaining otherwise applicable County protections.

  2. Rely on the County Comprehensive Plan

    Use the countywide plan as the principal planning framework for Keystone–Odessa.

  3. Decide with current data

    Crashes, traffic, emergency response, evacuation, flooding, wells, septic systems, accessibility, housing and environmental conditions.

  4. Hold open public meetings

    Let every affected resident take part before a decision is made.

What we are not asking

We are not asking to remove environmental, zoning, infrastructure or safety protections. Over one-third of the Community Plan area is mapped conservation land, lakes, reservoirs or ponds, and those protections exist separately from the Community Plan.

How we work

Evidence first. Sources beside every fact.

This site is built so you can check what we say for yourself.

Public records over opinion

We rely on County plans, GIS data, traffic counts, studies and official minutes, and show the original document wherever we can.

Sources next to the facts

Figures carry a short source line, with methods published on Sources & Methods: what was counted, and what was not.

Dated, and open to correction

Figures are tied to the date of their data. We aim to correct errors promptly and record changes in the Edits log.

Careful claims

We do not promise outcomes. Retiring the Plan is a change of framework; results still depend on County decisions made in public.
