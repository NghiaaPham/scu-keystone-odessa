  1. Oct. 16, 2026Evidence

    Area map now shows conservation land and water

    Our GIS measurement (Oct. 16, 2026) finds that 34.3% of the Community Plan area — 8,025 of 23,387 acres — is mapped conservation land, lakes, reservoirs or ponds. Those protections exist separately from the Community Plan.

    See the maps
  2. Oct. 2, 2026Parcel snapshot date

    Parcel snapshot: 4,542 improved single-family parcels

    Our analysis of the County's October 2 GIS parcel snapshot found that nearly half of these parcels (49.5%) report less than one acre. Infrastructure decisions should reflect the community already here.

    How we counted
  3. Sept. 24, 2026Petition

    Petition wording approved: version 3

    Version 3 of the petition adds the request to remove the Plan's associated policies and repeal Land Development Code Part 3.08.00 through the required public processes.
